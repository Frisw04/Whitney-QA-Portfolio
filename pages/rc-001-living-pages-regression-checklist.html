RC-001 — Living Pages Core Regression Checklist

Checklist ID
RC-001
Application
Living Pages
Test Type
Regression / Functional / Persistence
Platform
iOS / iPadOS
Test Environment
Physical Device
Overall Status
PASS

Purpose

Verify that core Living Pages functionality continues to work after code changes, bug fixes, persistence updates, layout changes, and multi-page feature work.

This checklist is intended to catch regressions across the areas most affected by recent development.

Launch & Navigation

  • ✓Application launches successfully.
  • ✓Current journal day loads correctly.
  • ✓Previous-day navigation works.
  • ✓Next-day navigation works when available.
  • ✓Same-day Page 1 / Page 2 navigation works.
  • ✓Correct page number is shown.
  • ✓Relaunch returns to the expected saved page state.

Text Editing

  • ✓Text can be entered and edited.
  • ✓Text remains aligned with the writing area.
  • ✓Text remains visible when the keyboard appears.
  • ✓Bottom writing area remains accessible.
  • ✓Portrait keyboard behavior works.
  • ✓Landscape keyboard behavior works.
  • ✓Text persists after page navigation.
  • ✓Text persists after force-close and relaunch.

PencilKit Drawing

  • ✓Drawing mode opens successfully.
  • ✓PencilKit strokes appear while drawing.
  • ✓Drawings remain visible after portrait → landscape rotation.
  • ✓Drawings remain visible after landscape → portrait rotation.
  • ✓Repeated rotation does not remove or shift drawings unexpectedly.
  • ✓Drawings remain associated with the correct page.
  • ✓Drawings persist after leaving and returning to the page.
  • ✓Drawings persist after force-close and relaunch.
  • ✓Hover and touch interaction do not corrupt drawing state.

Autosave & Saved State

  • ✓Editing triggers the saving state.
  • ✓Saved indicator updates after persistence completes.
  • ✓Text changes are saved.
  • ✓Drawing changes are saved.
  • ✓Page 1 saves independently.
  • ✓Page 2 saves independently.
  • ✓Saved content restores correctly after relaunch.

Multi-Page Persistence

  • ✓Page 1 retains its own text.
  • ✓Page 2 retains its own text.
  • ✓Page 1 retains its own drawings.
  • ✓Page 2 retains its own drawings.
  • ✓Page 1 retains its own attachments.
  • ✓Page 2 retains its own attachments.
  • ✓Content does not transfer between Page 1 and Page 2.
  • ✓Page content remains correct after navigation.
  • ✓Page content remains correct after force-close and relaunch.

Attachments & Media

  • ✓Photo attachment can be added.
  • ✓Video attachment can be added.
  • ✓Existing attachments remain visible.
  • ✓Attachments remain associated with the correct page.
  • ✓Attachments persist after page navigation.
  • ✓Attachments persist after force-close and relaunch.

Orientation & Layout

  • ✓Portrait layout renders correctly.
  • ✓Landscape layout renders correctly.
  • ✓Page geometry remains stable through rotation.
  • ✓Keyboard viewport remains usable in portrait.
  • ✓Keyboard viewport remains usable in landscape.
  • ✓Media and drawings remain positioned correctly after rotation.

Day & State Persistence

  • ✓Switching journal days loads the correct content.
  • ✓Returning to a previous day restores its content.
  • ✓Page-specific content does not appear on another day.
  • ✓Force-close and relaunch preserve saved journal state.
  • ✓Midnight/day transition does not remove persisted drawing state.

Regression Result

PASS

Core Living Pages functionality remained stable across the tested navigation, text editing, PencilKit drawing, autosave, keyboard, multi-page persistence, attachment, rotation, relaunch, and day-state scenarios.
